Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

imrdatabase.com ("the site") is a fan-made reference for the game Infinite Magicraid, run by Dubb (Dubb Gaming LLC). This page explains what information is collected when you visit.

Information we collect

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Championship Bracket Challenge. If you enter the Bracket Challenge, we store the in-game Player ID, player name and Discord name you type, your division and your picks. We use them only to run the challenge, show the leaderboard and contact winners about prizes. The leaderboard shows your player name and the last four digits of your Player ID; your picks are shown after entries lock. Your Discord name and full Player ID are only visible to the site owner. Entries are kept after each event ends so players can see their past results. To have an entry removed, contact us.

Accounts (Sign in with Discord). Accounts are optional. If you sign in with Discord, we receive and store only your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar. We do not receive your email address, password or server list. We use this to show your Bracket Challenge results on your My Account page, matched by the Discord name you entered on your brackets. A cookie keeps you signed in for up to 60 days. You can sign out at any time, and the "Delete my account" button removes your account and sign-in data.

The site may store small preferences in your browser's local storage (for example, a remembered setting). These stay on your device.

Advertising and cookies

The site may show ads served by third-party vendors, including Google. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to count visits and popular pages. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you.

Third-party links

The site links to YouTube and other sites. Their privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Dubb Gaming YouTube channel.
